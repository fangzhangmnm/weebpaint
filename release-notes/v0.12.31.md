## Keep drawing while your work uploads

Saving to OneDrive used to put up a full-screen "busy" overlay for a few seconds every time a
save was pushed to the cloud, and again when a file was renamed. During that window you could not draw.

This build routes those two background operations to a quiet notice instead. The upload still happens,
the sync badge in the top bar still shows the saving state, and you keep drawing the whole time.
Encrypting, emptying the trash, and folder operations still show the overlay — those need the document
held still.

## Apple Pencil pressure — a fallback for browsers that drop it

A tester on a 2023 iPad reported flat lines in WeebPaint while Procreate showed full pressure. WeebPaint
reads pressure from the high-frequency sample list Safari delivers with each pointer event; on that
device those samples appear to carry no pressure at all, so the whole stroke stayed at the pen-down value.

Now, when a sample has no pressure but the pointer event itself does, WeebPaint uses the event's
pressure. On browsers that report pressure correctly nothing changes. If your Pencil still draws flat
lines after this build, please tell us — that would mean the browser reports no pressure anywhere.

## Offline start is reliable again after a long idle

Browsers shut down an idle service worker after about half a minute and restart it on the next request.
After such a restart WeebPaint's worker had lost track of its own cache name, so every request opened a
fresh, empty cache and the precached files were never hit — whether the app could open offline came
down to luck. The worker now finds its cache again after a restart. It also no longer intercepts
requests that fall outside WeebPaint's own path.
