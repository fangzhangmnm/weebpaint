## Paintings open locked; unlock to keep painting

Paintings in your gallery now open view-only, so a stray touch can't change a finished piece. To paint, tap
"Keep editing" on the bar at the top of the canvas. The unlock is for that one painting on this device; it
runs out after a day, and painting on it keeps pushing that day back. When you're done, File → "Lock (done
editing for now)" saves your changes and locks it again. A file you open from your disk is locked the same
way and stays unlocked until you close it.

While a painting is locked you can still hide and show layers, make a selection and copy it, export, and use
Save As. Nothing is written into the file while it is locked.

## Gallery: star, tuck away, save a version

The ⋯ menu on a painting has three new items. "Star" protects a painting from deletion (remove the star
first to move it to the trash; emptying the trash keeps starred ones). "Tuck away" folds a painting into a
thin strip so the gallery stays tidy; tap it to open it back up. "Save a version" keeps a dated copy, shown
as "+N" on the painting's card.

Folders and paintings are now sorted together by name, and names that start with a date come newest first.
Images and other files sit in the same list.

## Saving is more careful

Saves now wait in line instead of skipping each other. This fixes two rare problems: pressing Save while an
automatic save was running could do nothing, and switching to another painting in the middle of an
automatic save could store the previous painting under the new one's name.

Coming back to the app no longer reports the painting you have open as "not uploaded" when it is only waiting
for its automatic upload.

## Export: upscale pixel art

The image export settings have "Upscale": Original size, ×2, ×3, ×4 or ×8. Each pixel becomes a sharp N×N
block, which is handy for posting pixel art. The choice is remembered per painting and is saved the next
time the painting is saved.

## Small things

- On Windows, Mac and Quest, sharing the debug log downloads a .txt file instead of opening the system share
  panel.
- After you lock encrypted paintings and forget the password, their gallery previews turn back into locks
  right away instead of after a reload.
