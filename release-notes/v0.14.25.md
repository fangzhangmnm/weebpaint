## Undo no longer loses its history after "Split colors" or "Merge group to layer"

Undoing right after **Split colors to layers** or **Merge group to layer** used to throw the whole
undo history away (the painting itself was never lost — only the ability to undo and redo). Fixed, with
a randomized stress test over layer operations guarding it.

## One-finger drawing is on by default

Drawing with a finger is now **on** out of the box. The first time the app sees a stylus it turns the
setting off for that device, once, and tells you. If you ever touch the switch yourself, the app keeps
your choice from then on and stops guessing.

## Reference window

The reference window moved into a shared component and gained a text card: pasting (Ctrl/Cmd+V) now
goes to whichever window has focus, and plain text or Markdown pasted into the reference window becomes
a card you can read next to your painting. Reference cards are saved in the file under their own
folder (`.weebpaint/references/`) with their file names, so the jump list can show them.

## Smaller things

- The layer **+** menu no longer stretches to the bottom of the screen.
- The clipping-mask items in the layer menu use the standard Chinese term (剪贴蒙版).
- Timelapse metadata is covered by the file-format migration table, so very old files load cleanly.
